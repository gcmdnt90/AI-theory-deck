Inside the model, around the model

Two things people constantly confuse.

The single most useful conceptual correction in the course. Almost every "can AI do X?" question is really a question about which side of this boundary X sits on.

The boundary

Inside — the processor

What the model learned during training. Fixed when you use it.

Around — the scaffolding

Memory, documents, tools, rules, loops. Built by you, changeable today.

The test: does it change the model itself, or only what the model is given?
The good news for an SME is entirely on the right-hand card: you cannot retrain a frontier model, and you do not need to. Everything that will make this useful for their business is "around" - and around is cheap, fast and reversible. Say that explicitly; it reframes the whole afternoon from "expensive AI project" to "configuration".

How a model is made

1. Reads textPredicts the next word across enormous amounts of writing.
2. Learns to follow instructionsExamples teach it to carry out a task instead of just continuing text.
3. Tuned by human feedbackPeople rate answers; the model is pushed toward the helpful ones.
The assistant you talk to is mostly made in steps 2 and 3.
Common belief in the room: "the model is one trained thing that knows facts". Correct it here. Step 3 also explains over-agreeableness, which returns in Module 6 - it was trained to please raters. Sources: Brown (2020); Ouyang (2022); Christiano (2017).

"Reasoning" — two different things

inside Trained

Deliberation was built into the model itself.

around Prompted

You ask an unchanged model to show its steps.

Same behaviour on screen, different mechanism underneath.
This is the slide the demo lands: the thinking toggle is the trained kind, "list your criteria first" is the prompted kind. Sources: Wei et al. (2022); Nye et al. (2021).

An agent = model + loop + tools + memory

LoopDecide, act, look at the result, decide again.
ToolsActions outside the model: files, search, email, databases.
MemoryWhat gets handed back to the model on the next turn.
The jump from chatbot to agent is mostly scaffolding, not a smarter model.
Everything from here is "around". Say the loop out loud - decide, act, observe - because you will narrate exactly those three words over the Cowork demo in Module 5, and they should recognise them when they hear them again. Sources: Yao et al. (2023) ReAct; Schick et al. (2023).
