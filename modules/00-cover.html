Language Models & Agents

What these systems are, how they work, and where they go wrong — with the tools open on screen.

San Marino Innovation & Università Politecnica delle Marche | 5 modules | 5 live demos | ~2h

The room is SME staff and owners with almost no exposure to the subject. The promise, in one line: by the end you can say what the machine is actually doing, and decide when to trust it.

Worth saying up front that every module ends with something running on screen: that promise is what buys attention during the theory.
